Bio

Welcome to my homepage!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Jinhe Center for Economic Research, Xi'an Jiaotong University. My research interests lie in applied microeconomics, development economics, and quantitative economic history. My current work focuses on structural transformation and gender-related issues in the Chinese context.
