Industrial policy and human capital accumulation of urban children: Evidence from the Third Front Construction in China

Weihua Yu, Zengyang Yu · Economic Modelling, Vol. 160 (2026), Article 107590 DOI

Using the Third Front Construction as a natural experiment, this paper asks whether the industrial relocation of the 1960s and 1970s raised the human capital of the children who grew up in the receiving cities.

Lighting up the future: The long-term effects of preschool exposure to electrification on female employment in rural China

Weihua Yu, Zengyang Yu, Xin Yao · Energy Economics, Vol. 161 (2026), Article 109496 DOI

Combining household survey data with the roll-out of rural electrification, this paper traces how having electricity at home before school age shapes women's later participation in the labour market.
