Industrial policy and human capital accumulation of urban children: Evidence from the Third Front Construction in China

Weihua Yu, Zengyang Yu · Economic Modelling, Vol. 160 (2026), Article 107590 DOI

Cities that received Third Front factories saw the children who grew up there stay in school longer. The finding reframes industrial policy as an investment in people, not just in plants.

Lighting up the future: The long-term effects of preschool exposure to electrification on female employment in rural China

Weihua Yu, Zengyang Yu, Xin Yao · Energy Economics, Vol. 161 (2026), Article 109496 DOI

Women who had electricity at home before school age were markedly more likely to work outside the home as adults. The headline is that the timing of infrastructure at home, not just its arrival, shapes a woman's lifetime of work.
